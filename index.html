Yingshu CHEN completed her PhD in Computer Science and Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) supervised by Sai-Kit Yeung and Ajay Joneja. Her research interests cover computer vision and computer graphics for computational design such as 2D and 3D style transfer. Yingshu received her B.Eng in Digital Media Technology from Zhejiang University and M.Sc in Computer Science from The University of Hong Kong.

360VOTS: Visual Object Tracking and Segmentation in Omnidirectional Videos

Arxiv 2024.

[Project] [Arxiv] [Toolkit]
